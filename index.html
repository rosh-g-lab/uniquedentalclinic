Prosthodontics & Aesthetic Restoration

Classification by Fabrication & Layering Design

Under the clinical guidance of Dr. Bibek Khanal (Consultant Prosthodontist & Implantologist) and Dr. Barun Giri, our clinic provides precision German CAD/CAM Zirconia restorations customized to your masticatory bite forces and smile aesthetics.

Maximum Strength

Monolithic Zirconia

Fabrication & Design:

Milled from a single, solid block of pure zirconia with no added surface porcelain.

Advantages (Pros):
  • Extremely durable with up to 1200+ MPa flexural load strength.
  • Virtually chip-resistant — cannot fracture or delaminate.
  • Requires minimal tooth reduction, conserving healthy tooth enamel.
Considerations (Cons):
  • Less translucent than natural enamel (though newer 4Y/5Y grades look much better).
Best Clinical Application:

Posterior teeth (molars & premolars), heavy chewers, and patients with bruxism (nighttime teeth clenching).

Aesthetic Perfection

Layered Zirconia

Fabrication & Design:

A high-strength zirconia core (coping) hand-layered with softer, more aesthetic dental porcelain on top.

Advantages (Pros):
  • Superior, custom-tailored front-tooth aesthetics and opalescence.
  • Individual shade characterization matching adjacent natural teeth flawlessly.
Considerations (Cons):
  • The outer porcelain layer is prone to chipping or cracking under heavy biting forces.
Best Clinical Application:

Anterior smile zone (front incisors & canines) where natural light reflection and cosmetic beauty are paramount.

Gold Standard • Gradient

Fabrication & Design:

A single blank engineered with a vertical gradient—combining high-strength 3Y at the base/neck transitioning into high-translucency 4Y or 5Y at the biting edge.

Advantages (Pros):
  • Combines strong foundation support with a natural shade and translucency gradient.
  • Zero risk of outer porcelain chipping (solid monolithic structure with continuous multi-phase density).
Considerations (Cons):
  • Requires advanced high-precision multi-axis CAD/CAM sintering technology.
Best Clinical Application:

Both anterior and posterior restorations requiring the ideal harmony of robust strength and lifelike smile aesthetics.
